Q1: Why was RL chosen over hand-written if-then movement rules?
YOUR ANSWER: RL lets the computer explore/learn the geometry itself
  instead of manually controlling all 9 joints via IK/FK equations.
GRADE: Right instinct, two corrections -- (a) the report's real
  contrast is RL vs hand-written IF-THEN RULES, not RL vs IK/FK
  (IK is later actually combined WITH RL in Section 6, not an
  alternative to it); (b) the specific reason is that with 9
  independently controllable joints, the number of (position x
  orientation x current configuration) combinations you'd need to
  write a rule for is too large to enumerate by hand.

Q2: What do actor and critic do in SAC, and why entropy regularisation?
YOUR ANSWER: actor = network proposing/taking actions from current
  state; critic = judges how good the action is via predicted
  reward/closeness to goal; entropy = stops the network tunnel-
  visioning on one fixed answer, keeps exploring alternatives.
GRADE: Correct on all three parts. No corrections.

Q3: Phase 2 success conditions + what the drift penalty does.
YOUR ANSWER (refined): success = distance below ~0.02m AND facing
  angle within a certain radian range, both at once. Drift penalty
  = enters target position range, leaves it while exploring
  alternates, then re-enters -- the penalty exists so it isn't
  "finishing and milking the bonus rewards" instead of actually
  completing the task.
GRADE: Correct, both parts, after refinement. Exact numbers for the
  record: distance < 0.02m AND angle < 1.2 rad (both, together).
  Drift penalty is a fixed one-time -10, triggered the instant the
  arm exits the position gate (0.03m) having been inside it the
  step before -- a trip-wire on LEAVING, not a distance-scaled
  ongoing punishment.

Q4: Sec 3.3/4 "position presumed correct" -- what's done to the
starting joints, and why does it guarantee position is already right?
YOUR ANSWER: joints displaced so the end-effector starts within
  target space, then joints 6-9 re-randomised so RL can learn
  orientation "to form the correct position again."
GRADE: First half correct. Second half corrected in chat: position
  is never "re-formed" -- it's never disturbed in the first place.
  Joints 0-5 (the links between them) are copied unchanged from a
  real valid configuration and never move. Joints 6-9 form a
  "spherical wrist" -- their rotation axes all intersect at the
  end-effector point itself (like a camera gimbal mount), so
  spinning them re-aims the probe without being able to drag its
  tip away from that point. That mechanical fact is WHY scrambling
  6-9 only breaks orientation, never position.
